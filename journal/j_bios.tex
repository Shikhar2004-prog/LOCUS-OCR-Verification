\bio{bios/dubey.jpg}{Shikhar Dubey}{is a researcher in the Intelligent Systems group of the School of Computing and
Electrical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Mandi, Himachal Pradesh, India. His research addresses the
reliability of optical character recognition, in particular OCR verification with graph neural networks and
unsupervised methods for low-resolution text. He has published
work on multi-feature graph convolution networks for Hindi OCR verification at the BHASHA workshop (2025) and on
unsupervised confirmation of low-resolution OCR at the Mind, Brain, and Consciousness Conference (2025). ORCID: 0009-0001-9035-4392. E-mail: s24130@students.iitmandi.ac.in.}

\bio{bios/saxena.jpg}{Sanhita Saxena}{is an M.Tech Research Scholar in the School of Mathematical and Statistical
Sciences, Indian Institute of Technology Mandi, Himachal Pradesh, India. Her research focuses on label-efficient verification of optical character recognition
with graph neural networks: she is the lead author of ``Reading Between the Characters: Label-Efficient OCR Verification
with Graph Neural Networks'' (NICCAI 2026, Hyderabad), which models each word as a relational line graph of
real-versus-rendered character comparisons and reaches near fully supervised accuracy from 25 labelled examples per
class. Her interests include few-shot learning, graph representation learning and document image analysis. Before
her research degree she trained as a full-stack (MERN) web developer. E-mail: s25008@students.iitmandi.ac.in.}
